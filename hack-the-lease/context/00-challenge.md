# The Challenge — Hack the Lease

- **Host:** Hackberry Lane (operator of Haven on Geddes, Ann Arbor)
- **Ask:** Build a *working technical tool* that helps students choose housing based on **how they actually live** — schedule, budget, roommates, habits — **not demographics**.
- **Deadline:** Fri Nov 20, 5:00 PM (moved up from 6 PM), Ross R0230
- **Format:** 5 min demo + 5 min Q&A

## Judging rubric
| Weight | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 35% | It works (functioning, technical tool) |
| 30% | Problem framing & product choices |
| 20% | Decision quality, evidence & assumptions |
| 15% | UX |

## Hard requirements
- Demo must show **decision sensitivity**: change one input live → the recommendation changes.
- Any synthetic data (listings, student profiles) must be **clearly labeled as synthetic** on screen.

- Reid Marks — operates leases (cares about real leasing pain)
- Asher Lin — background in AI agents that flag issues in documents
